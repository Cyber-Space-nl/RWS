Interpretation

  • RWS and Nginx both sustained more than 99% of the offered rate in all accepted runs with zero failed responses.
  • RWS median p99 latency was 7.10 ms, 13.3% lower than Nginx at 8.19 ms. Median throughput differed by only 0.33% because both were constrained by the 10,000 RPS offer.
  • Apache saturated well below the offered rate: median throughput was 2,723.67 RPS and the three runs recorded 250 failed responses in total. Latency percentiles include only completed responses and therefore understate Apache's failure impact.
  • Measured maximum RSS was approximately 254.7 MiB for the already-running RWS process, 11.9 MiB for all Nginx processes and 95.1 MiB for Apache. These are total process measurements, not per-connection allocation deltas.
  • The 100 ms CPU sampler has 10 percentage-point resolution on this one-vCPU host; CPU figures are directional rather than precise enough for small percentage claims.
